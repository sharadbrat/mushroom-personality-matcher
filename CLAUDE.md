# CLAUDE.md

Guidance for working in this repo.

## Project

Shroomarium — a React + Vite gallery of clay mushroom characters (`src/data/mushrooms.ts`, images in `src/data/images/`). Browse/search, take a personality quiz for a match percentage, or use "Find my mushroom" to pick traits and get ranked matches. Installable offline-first PWA, deployed to https://shroomarium.netlify.app.

## Content guidelines

When adding a mushroom, or writing/editing its personality traits, description, or card image framing, follow [MUSHROOM_CONTENT_GUIDE.md](./MUSHROOM_CONTENT_GUIDE.md) — it covers the fixed 30-trait vocabulary, the "exactly 3 traits, no duplicate sets" rule, description voice/length, and how card framing overrides work.
